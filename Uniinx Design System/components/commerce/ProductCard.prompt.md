ProductCard is the repeating storefront grid tile — placeholder photo, item label, tracked price, and a "Buy it →" pill. Use it for any product grid on the Uniinx storefront.

```jsx
<ProductCard label="Tshirt" price="R . 200" image="/assets/tee.jpg" />
```

Notable props: `image` (omit to show the warm-ivory placeholder), `width`/`height` (defaults match the storefront's 388×527 card). Price renders in the terracotta accent; a subtle Kalamkari-style corner flourish sits on the photo (v2 heritage motif).
